## Milestones

1. **M0 — Readiness:** G0/G1 complete before launch proposal
2. **M1 — Measurement control:** tracking, baseline, claim-safe control assets
3. **M2 — Proposition shortlist:** primary + secondary territory from concept/voice data
4. **M3 — Mid-trial review:** competitor/voice/creative/channel evidence reconciled
5. **M4 — Pilot economics:** conservative contribution and operational readiness
6. **M5 — End-trial decision:** no-go, iterate/pilot 2, or separate scale approval request

## Experiment backlog priority

Score 1–5 on impact, evidence gain, cost, speed, compliance risk; unknown remains unknown. Priority order:
1. Product truth/claim validation
2. Sensory and willingness-to-pay test
3. Ritual vs proof-transparency proposition
4. Meta/TikTok hook/format
5. Marketplace/PDP conversion
6. Creator cohort and rights reuse
7. Retail/clinic sampling
8. Bundle/repeat mechanism after economics

## RACI

| Workstream | Owner | Marketing | Product/Reg | Finance | Analytics | Ops/Partner | Legal/Privacy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Product truth/claim | A | C | R | I | I | C | C |
| Budget/spend | A | R | C | R | C | I | I |
| Creative/content | A | R | R/C | I | C | C | C |
| Tracking/analysis | A | C | C | C | R | C | C |
| Retail/clinic pilot | A | C | R/C | C | C | R | C |
| Adverse/complaint | A | I | R | I | C | R | C |
| Stop/scale | A | R | R | R | R | C | C |

A=Accountable, R=Responsible, C=Consulted, I=Informed; ชื่อบุคคลยังไม่ถูกกำหนด

## Governance artifacts

- Product Evidence Pack + claim matrix
- assumptions/unknowns register
- asset/version/rights register
- experiment brief/result log
- weekly decision log + reserve release record
- incident/correction/adverse escalation log
- finance reconciliation and cohort report

## Final decision rubric

- Customer demand signal
- competitor whitespace
- credible brand right-to-win
- contribution/channel viability
- content repeatability
- defensibility
- compliance risk
- operations/capacity

คะแนนเป็น rubric ช่วยตัดสิน ไม่ใช่ measured market fact และ scale ต้องผ่าน owner approval boundary ใหม่
