# Step 12 — Executive Report: Premium Toothpaste Thailand Trial

> Product: ยาสีฟันพรีเมียม — แบรนด์ทดลอง
> Owner input: retail price 350 THB/unit
> Planning period: 3 months

> Geography: Thailand | Evidence retrieval: 2026-08-28

## 1. Executive Summary

Public evidence does not support a precise premium-toothpaste TAM/SAM. Two Thai trade articles report total toothpaste value directionally around 10.2bn THB (2024) and ~12bn THB (2026), but disclose no retail-audit methodology; premium SAM and brand SOM remain unquantified. The competitive audit covers exactly 10 brands and shows a wide observed price ladder from mass-premium to imported specialty. A 350 THB unit can occupy a useful bridge only after pack size, formula, proof and economics are known

The directional Customer Voice corpus contains exactly 100 de-identified public records. Dominant coded themes are trust/proof 74%, whitening expectations 63%, price/value 46%, adverse-experience language 33%, sensitivity 27%, packaging 21% and taste 18%. These are not population rates: 66/100 records belong to Colgate Optic White and 60 come from one public review source. The strategic implication is not to copy whitening intensity. It is to test **Premium daily ritual × proof transparency** as the primary winning zone, with gentle-brightening/comfort only as a secondary concept territory until formula/test/Thai claim review support it

The proposed all-channel budget covers Meta, TikTok, Google/YouTube, LINE, SEO/owned, marketplaces, creator/KOL, PR, retail/clinic sampling, events and reserve. Allocation sums exactly to 3m THB. Scenarios are explicit arithmetic illustrations—not forecasts—and the Base illustration produces only 0.581x revenue/total-plan spend. Because COGS, margin, VAT, fees, discounts and returns are unknown, allowable CAC/commercial break-even cannot yet be set. Recommended action is a proof-first validation pilot, not scale launch

## 3. Strategic decisions by Step

### Step 1 — Market

Do not claim an audited premium TAM. Define premium by unit price + claim/benefit + channel + pack, then obtain current SKU census/paid audit. Use omnichannel behavior as a test hypothesis: social/creator for discovery, search/owned proof, marketplace/D2C conversion lab, retail/clinic/sampling for trust and sensorial feedback

### Step 2 — Competition

Fixed set: DENTISTE', Marvis, APAGARD, CURAPROX Enzycal, VUSSEN, Sensodyne, Colgate Optic White, Oral-B/Crest 3D White, Sparkle and Twin Lotus. Mass alternatives anchor low prices/access; imported brands bring design or technology; specialist brands bring proof systems. Gap = inspectable proof + sensorial ritual + Thai-accessible selective premium—not “strongest whitening”

### Step 3 — SWOT/TOWS

Real strengths are agility, price/budget clarity and ability to build governance from zero. Formula/proof/margin/capacity are unknown and cannot be called strengths. TOWS therefore prioritizes Product Evidence Pack, blind sensory/WTP test, economics, claim governance and limited pilots

### Step 4 — Customer

Use three need-state persona hypotheses:
1. Ritual & Proof Upgrader — 60% test focus
2. Appearance Seeker, Evidence First — 30%
3. Ingredient & Comfort Controller — 10%

These are focus allocations, not market shares. Recruit future Thai research by behavior/need rather than unsupported demographics

### Step 5 — Winning Zone/Positioning

Primary provisional: **Premium daily ritual × proof transparency**
Secondary experiment: **Gentle-brightening experience without overclaim**
Tagline territory: **“พรีเมียมที่พิสูจน์ได้”** (brand-process promise, not efficacy claim)

No-go: disease treatment/prevention, instant/guaranteed whitening, unsupported sensitivity/antibacterial/natural/fluoride/clinical/dentist/certification/safety language, fake before-after/reviews/scarcity

### Step 6 — Online

Use online as learning system. Meta 900k, TikTok 540k, Google/YT 144k, LINE 126k, SEO/owned/marketplace 90k. Establish controls first, require winner replication and conservative contribution before scale. Asset register must connect product version, evidence ID, wording, rights and approver

### Step 7 — Offline/O2O

360k allocation: retail sampling 120k, clinic pilot 90k, small events 60k, PR asset 45k, trade materials 30k, O2O tracking 15k. Clinic context is not endorsement. QR/UTM/location-cell cohort must connect sample→scan→feedback→purchase→repeat. OOH is deferred

### Step 8 — Content

12-week system: Awareness 1–4, Consideration 5–8, Conversion 9–12. Exactly 30 scripts are supplied, each with hook, spoken body, CTA, caption, production note, KPI hypothesis and claim/compliance note. Since efficacy evidence is absent, angles use education, lifestyle, proof transparency and honest unknowns

### Step 10 — Measurement

Business truth requires order revenue minus fees/discount/returns/COGS—not platform ROAS alone. Reporting: daily safety/delivery, twice-weekly creative, weekly stop/iterate/scale, monthly finance reconciliation, end-trial no-go/pilot2/scale request. Adverse/claim mismatch triggers immediate stop

### Step 11 — Execution

W-2→W0 closes product truth/economics/protocol/tracking/approval. W1–4 establishes controls and proposition shortlist; W5–8 validates proof/sensory/channel cohorts; W9–12 tests tracked conversion/repeat and prepares decision. No forced spend at month end

## 5. 30/60/90-day proposed action plan

### Days 1–30 — Truth and control

1. Owner/Product complete formula, pack, notification, label, claim and test dossier
2. Finance/Ops complete COGS, gross-to-net, fees, returns, MOQ, capacity and SLA
3. Product/Research run owner-approved blind sensorial + WTP protocol
4. Marketing/Regulatory lock message house, claim matrix and asset register
5. Analytics QA UTM/QR/order/CRM/consent and baseline
6. Produce control assets from 30 scripts; no publication until owner approval
7. Decision M1: no-go if G0/G1 incomplete

### Days 31–60 — Proposition and pilot

1. Test ritual vs proof-transparency concepts with controlled cohorts
2. Test Meta/TikTok hooks; search/owned proof page; marketplace/PDP order
3. Run limited retail/clinic/sampling only after product/safety/consent approval
4. Code Thai primary feedback using same taxonomy as public Voice
5. Reconcile price objection, pack usability, taste/foam/after-feel and complaint
6. Refresh competitor prices/claims and regulatory status
7. Decision M3: choose primary/secondary/no-go; no national scale

### Days 61–90 — Economics, repeat and owner decision

1. Test tracked conversion only within allowable CAC
2. Measure D7/D30 experience, repeat intent/purchase and complaint closure
3. Test bundle/replenishment only when pack/usage/margin support it
4. Require ≥2 cohort/creative replications before reserve release
5. Reconcile order revenue, fees, returns, inventory and contribution
6. Produce end-trial evidence/assumption/correction report
7. Owner chooses no-go, iterate/pilot 2, or separate limited-scale approval

## 6. Risk and mitigation

| Risk | Material impact | Mitigation / stop rule |
|---|---|---|
| Formula/claim/label mismatch | regulatory/trust harm | Product Evidence Pack + Thai review; stop asset |
| Whitening/sensitivity overclaim | health-adjacent risk | secondary concept only; evidence/wording gate |
| Customer adverse pattern | safety/reputation | stop cohort, preserve lot/version, escalate |
| 350 THB fails WTP | low conversion/discount spiral | pack/value test before scale |
| Negative contribution | cash destruction despite revenue | allowable CAC + conservative returns/fees |
| Fake/variant marketplace confusion | trust/returns | official channel/version/lot clarity |
| Creator overclaim/rights issue | compliance/content loss | approved script, disclosure, usage rights, version log |
| Voice sample bias | wrong positioning | Thai primary research; denominator/source split |
| Stock/capacity failure | wasted media/poor experience | SLA/fill-rate gate; no reserve release |
| Tracking/attribution inflation | false scale decision | finance reconciliation + holdout when possible |

## 7. Compliance gate before any publication

1. Exact product is notified/allowed for sale/import and current ingredient rules pass
2. Thai label and fluoride-specific rule (if applicable) pass qualified review
3. Every claim has product/version/method/evidence/approved wording and limitation
4. PDP, ads, hashtags, creators, testimonials, before-after, live selling and PR all match
5. Price/promotion/gift/COD/return/stock conditions are complete; lucky draw separately reviewed
6. Consent/privacy/complaint/adverse process and owner are live
7. Asset has approver/timestamp/expiry; correction/pause mechanism tested

## 8. Success criteria for the trial—not campaign promise

Success means:
- G0 product truth and G1 economics complete
- primary territory wins replicated concept/sensory tests
- contribution/order is positive under conservative fees/returns
- claim incident zero and adverse escalation works
- tracked conversion and repeat signal justify next pilot
- owner has enough evidence to say no-go as confidently as scale

No fixed sales/ROAS target is committed before allowable CAC and baseline exist
