## 9.5 Break-even inputs

Revenue-only recovery of marketing spend would require 3,000,000 / 350 = 8,571.43, rounded up in practice to at least 8,572 units. This is **not commercial break-even** because the following are unknown:

- COGS and packaging
- gross margin and wholesale/retail margin
- VAT treatment
- marketplace/payment/fulfillment fees
- discounts, samples, creator seeding, returns and refunds
- fixed overhead and inventory financing

Allowable CAC formula:
`net selling price − COGS − variable channel fees − fulfillment − expected returns − target contribution`

Until those inputs exist, any ROAS or sales target is an illustration only

## 9.6 Reserve release rule

Release 25–50k increments only when (1) claim and product version pass, (2) winner replicates in ≥2 cohorts, (3) contribution/order is non-negative under conservative fees/returns, (4) stock/SLA pass, and (5) owner approves spend. Otherwise reserve stays unspent
